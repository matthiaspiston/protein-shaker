Questions

Before you ask.

Does it make shakes taste metallic?

No. Titanium is one of the most inert metals there is, which is why surgeons put it inside people. It doesn’t react with whey, milk, fruit acids or creatine, and it doesn’t pass on any taste.

Will it keep my shake cold?

It’s single-wall to keep the weight at 196 g, so it doesn’t insulate like a vacuum flask. A few ice cubes will hold a shake cold through a normal session.

How does the orb mix without a whisk?

The orb is a solid 40 mm sphere. It’s heavy enough to punch through clumps and round enough that nothing gets trapped on it. Eight to ten shakes is enough for a standard scoop.

Can I put hot drinks in it?

Yes, up to 100 °C. The body gets hot to the touch because it isn’t insulated. Don’t shake anything hot with the cap closed; steam builds pressure.

Will the colour on Bronze and Aurora wear off?

Anodized colour is the metal’s own oxide, so it can’t peel. Heavy abrasion can scratch through it, and a scratch shows raw titanium underneath. Obsidian uses a DLC coating that is harder still.

Where do you ship?

Worldwide from our warehouses in Portland and Rotterdam. Shipping is free on orders over $150; below that it’s a flat $8.
